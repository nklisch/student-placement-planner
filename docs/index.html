Built for school staff

Works like a spreadsheet

Type into tables, paste from Excel or Google Sheets, or import a CSV file. If you can use a spreadsheet, you can use this.

Fairness you choose

Spread drive times fairly, keep total driving low, or honor student choices first. Every rule you set is respected.

How it works

  1. 1

    Add your students

    Type, paste, or import a list. Names are enough for manual driving times; maps use addresses or coordinates.

  2. 2

    Add your locations

    Enter each placement site and how many students it can take.

  3. 3

    Add rules if needed

    Keep students together or apart, pin a placement, or set a maximum drive. Most projects need few rules.

  4. 4

    Provide driving times

    Paste minutes, use the no-key community service, connect openrouteservice or Google, or download a region for fully offline use.

  5. 5

    Find placements

    Review the complete list, drive times, road distances, and capacity use. Then export or print it.

What's happening behind the scenes

The app compares the possible ways of matching students to locations. It sets aside any arrangement that breaks a capacity or rule, then chooses the one that best matches the goal you selected. For school-sized groups this normally takes seconds, and a rule is never quietly bent to make the result look better.

Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Student Placement Planner is open source under the MIT license — free to use, share, and modify.

Where does student data go?

The app has no app accounts, cloud database, or telemetry. Project files stay on your computer. If you choose an online route service, only street addresses or coordinates are sent — never names, IDs, choices, capacities, or rules.

Can it work without internet?

Yes. Enter driving times yourself, or download an OpenStreetMap region directly from Geofabrik in the app. The app prepares it locally; later address matching and road searches run entirely on your computer.

Do I need to be good with computers?

No. Download the installer above and open it like a normal program. Because this beta has preview signatures, follow the one-time confirmation instructions shown in the download section.

Does it work on Windows or Mac?

The beta supports Windows x64 and Apple-Silicon Mac. An Intel Mac build is not currently available.

How many students can it handle?

It is designed for up to about 100 students, which covers a typical school placement run.
